If you only experience one Jewish holiday in Tel Aviv, make it Purim. The whole city dresses up, from kids on the way to school to office workers and bar staff, and the streets turn into one big party.

[[holiday: Purim]]

## What happens

- **Costumes everywhere**, for days before the holiday itself.
- **Street parties** across the city, with the biggest crowds in the centre and Florentin.
- **Hamantaschen** (oznei Haman), triangular filled cookies, in every bakery.
- **Kids' parades and school events** in the morning.
