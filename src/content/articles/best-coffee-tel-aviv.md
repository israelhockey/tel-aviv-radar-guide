Café culture is the heartbeat of Tel Aviv. People meet, work and break up over coffee here, and the specialty scene has exploded. Order an "hafuch" (the local cappuccino) or go for a filter at the roasters.

## Our picks

[[pick: Cafelix | One of the city's best-known specialty roasters, with beans you'll find in cafés all over town. Great espresso and a good place to buy a bag.]]

[[pick: Nahat Cafe | Serious coffee in a relaxed space. A favourite for filter coffee and a quiet morning.]]

[[pick: Loveat | Organic coffee and a big all-day menu. The kind of place you can settle into with a laptop.]]

## Good to know

- An "hafuch" is a cappuccino-style milky coffee. "Botz" (mud) is the old-school Turkish-style coffee.
- Cafés open early, often by 7:00, and many neighbourhood places close in the afternoon.
- Working from a café is normal, but order regularly if you stay for hours.
