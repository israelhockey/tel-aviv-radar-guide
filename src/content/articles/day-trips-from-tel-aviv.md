Israel is small. From Tel Aviv you can be in Jerusalem's Old City for lunch, float in the Dead Sea in the afternoon, or walk Roman ruins by the sea and be back for dinner. Before you go anywhere, check your government's current travel advice for the region.

## Jerusalem

**How far:** around 35 minutes by fast train from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem's Yitzhak Navon station, or about an hour by car or bus.

The Old City's four quarters, the Western Wall, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, the Mahane Yehuda market and the Israel Museum. One day only scratches the surface, so pick a focus: the Old City in the morning and the market in the afternoon is a classic.

[[tip: Jerusalem is quieter and more religious than Tel Aviv, and almost everything closes on Shabbat. Go Sunday to Thursday, and cover shoulders and knees for holy sites.]]

## The Dead Sea and Masada

**How far:** around 1.5 to 2 hours by car.

Float in the saltiest sea on earth at one of the public beaches, cover yourself in mud, and combine it with Masada, Herod's mountain-top fortress, or the springs at Ein Gedi. Start very early in summer: it's one of the hottest places on the planet.

## Caesarea

**How far:** around an hour north by car.

A Roman harbour city on the sea, with a restored amphitheatre, a hippodrome and an aqueduct on the beach. Good for families, and easy to combine with the wineries around Zichron Ya'akov.

## Haifa

**How far:** around an hour to an hour and a half by train.

Israel's third city climbs Mount Carmel above the bay. The terraced Bahá'í Gardens are the main sight, and the German Colony below them is good for lunch.

## Akko (Acre)

**How far:** about 1.5 hours by train.

A UNESCO-listed old city of Crusader halls, Ottoman walls, a bustling market and a fishing port. Easy to pair with Haifa on the same day.

## Getting there

- **Trains** are the easiest way to reach Jerusalem, Haifa and Akko. They don't run on Shabbat.
- **Rental cars** make the Dead Sea, Masada, Caesarea and the north much easier. You don't need one in Tel Aviv itself.
- **Guided day tours** pick you up in Tel Aviv and handle the logistics, especially useful for the Dead Sea and Masada.
