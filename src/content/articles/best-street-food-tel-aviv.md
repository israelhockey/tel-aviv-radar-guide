Some of the best food in Tel Aviv comes in a pita. It's fast, cheap and eaten standing up, and every local has a strong opinion about which stand is the best.

## The essentials

- **Falafel**: fried chickpea balls in pita with salads and tahini.
- **Sabich**: fried aubergine, hard-boiled egg, potato, salads and amba (a tangy mango sauce). An Iraqi-Jewish breakfast turned street-food star.
- **Shawarma**: slow-roasted meat (often turkey or lamb) carved off the spit.

## Our picks

[[pick: Miznon | Chef Eyal Shani's famous pita bar. Stuffed pitas, roasted whole cauliflower and a loud, happy room.]]

[[pick: Sabich Frishman | A tiny stand that many say makes the best sabich in town. Expect a queue at lunchtime.]]

[[pick: Falafel HaKosem | "The Magician" is famous for its falafel and its friendly staff, who often hand you a falafel ball to try while you wait.]]

[[pick: Ovad's Sabich | A sabich specialist with a cult following. Rich, messy and worth it.]]
