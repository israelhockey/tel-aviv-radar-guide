Tel Aviv is a great city for kids: safe, walkable and full of playgrounds, with the beach never far away.

## Outdoors

- **Beaches** with playgrounds and calm water. Our [beach page](/beach) shows which beaches have playgrounds and facilities.
- **Yarkon Park**: paddle boats, lawns, bike paths and big playgrounds.
- **Playgrounds** in almost every neighbourhood, many with shade.

## Rainy or hot days

- **Museums**: the Tel Aviv Museum of Art runs family activities, and several museums have kids' programmes.
- **Community centres** run workshops and classes, often free or cheap.
