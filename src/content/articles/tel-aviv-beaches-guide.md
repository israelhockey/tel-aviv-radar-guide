Tel Aviv has 14 kilometres of coastline and more than a dozen official beaches, each with its own crowd. Here's a quick guide, north to south. For today's sea temperature, waves, jellyfish reports and the best hours, see our live [beach page](/beach).

## North

- **Tel Baruch**: wide, calm and family-friendly, with lots of parking.
- **Metzitzim**: popular with families, with a playground and the port nearby.
- **Nordau (the religious beach)**: separate days for men and women, and very quiet.
- **Hilton**: surfers, a dog beach and a popular gay beach section.

## Central

- **Gordon**: lively, with a saltwater pool, beach volleyball and cafés.
- **Frishman**: matkot, sunsets and the classic Tel Aviv beach scene.
- **Bograshov and Jerusalem Beach**: central and busy, a short walk from the city's hotels.
- **Banana Beach and the Drummers' Beach**: known for drum circles at sunset on Fridays.

## South

- **Alma**: a little rougher and more local, close to Neve Tzedek.
- **Jaffa (Givat Aliya)**: rocky in places, with views of old Jaffa.

[[tip: Swim only when a lifeguard is on duty and check the flag at the tower: white is safe, red is dangerous, black means no swimming.]]
