Most people in Tel Aviv speak English, which is both a blessing and a trap: it's easy to never learn Hebrew. Even a little Hebrew changes how the city feels, from ordering coffee to dealing with your landlord.

## Ulpan: the classic route

An ulpan is an intensive Hebrew course. New immigrants are usually entitled to a subsidised ulpan through the state. In Tel Aviv, **Ulpan Gordon** is one of the oldest and best known, and there are many private ulpans too.

- **Intensive courses** meet most mornings for several months.
- **Evening courses** suit people who work.
- **Online classes** are popular for the basics before you arrive.

## Words you'll hear every day

- **Sababa**: cool, great.
- **Yalla**: come on, let's go.
- **Achla**: excellent.
- **Rega**: one moment.
- **Toda**: thank you. **Bevakasha**: please, or you're welcome.
