Tel Aviv's chef restaurants are lively rather than stiff: open kitchens, loud music, sharing plates and staff who'll tell you what to order. These are the places to book for a night to remember.

## Our picks

[[pick: Taizu | Asian street food reimagined in a dramatic dining room near Sarona. Share as much as you can.]]

[[pick: Claro | Set in a restored Templer building in Sarona, with a big wood-fired kitchen and seasonal Israeli produce.]]

[[pick: HaAchim | "The Brothers" serves big, bold Middle Eastern sharing dishes in a buzzing room on Ibn Gvirol.]]

[[pick: DOK | A small, chef-driven restaurant cooking with local, seasonal ingredients. Intimate and inventive.]]

[[pick: Herzl 16 | A stylish bar-restaurant that moves from dinner to drinks as the night goes on.]]

[[tip: The big names fill up days ahead, especially on Thursdays and Saturdays. Bar seats are often easier to get.]]
