Tel Aviv takes its burgers seriously. Butchers grind their own blends, buns come from local bakeries, and a good number of the best places stay open well past midnight. Here's where we'd send a friend.

## Our picks

[[pick: Vitrina | A small counter with a big reputation. Short menu, juicy patties, and a queue that tells you everything. Order at the window and eat on the pavement like everyone else.]]

[[pick: Moses | A proper sit-down burger bar that works for groups and dates alike. Big menu, cocktails, and late hours make it an easy end to a night out.]]

[[pick: Benz Brothers | Run by people who know their meat. The burgers are thin, crisp-edged and properly seasoned, with a no-nonsense menu.]]

[[pick: Prozdor | Small and unfussy, with burgers that punch above the size of the room. Good for a quick, excellent lunch.]]

[[pick: Agadir | An Israeli classic. Plenty of choice, generous portions and long opening hours: reliable when you're hungry at an odd hour.]]

[[tip: Most burger places in town ask how you'd like it cooked. "Medium" here usually means properly pink in the middle.]]

## Good to know

- Plenty of burger spots are kosher and close on Friday afternoon. Look for the kosher tag on each place page.
- Late at night, the centre around Dizengoff and Allenby has the most choice.
- Veggie and vegan burgers are everywhere. See our [vegan guide](/magazine/best-vegan-tel-aviv).
