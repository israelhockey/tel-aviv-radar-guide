December in Tel Aviv is a mix: Hanukkah lights, Christmas in Jaffa, and a New Year's Eve that's a regular working day but still a big night out.

## Christmas in Jaffa

Jaffa has a historic Christian community, and its churches, including **St Peter's Church** at Kedumim Square, mark Christmas with services and decorations. Jaffa's clock tower area and old city often have festive events and markets in December. Check [What's on](/events) for this year's events.

## Christmas Day

25 December is a normal working day in Israel. Shops, restaurants and offices are open, and some hotels and restaurants offer Christmas dinners.

## Hanukkah

Hanukkah usually falls in December too, with candles in windows, doughnuts ("sufganiyot") everywhere and events for families. See our [Hanukkah guide](/magazine/hanukkah-tel-aviv).

## New Year's Eve ("Sylvester")

Israelis call New Year's Eve "Sylvester". It isn't a public holiday, and 1 January is a working day, but bars and clubs throw big parties. Book ahead for restaurants, and expect queues at popular clubs.

- Party listings: [What's on](/events?cat=party) and our [nightlife guide](/magazine/nightlife-guide-tel-aviv).
- If 31 December falls on a Friday, Shabbat affects transport. Check [Shabbat times](/shabbat).
