Tel Aviv isn't cheap, but many of its best experiences are free.

## Always free

- **The beaches**, with free showers and changing rooms at most lifeguard stations. See [today's beach conditions](/beach).
- **The promenade**, 14 km of seafront from Tel Baruch to Jaffa, perfect for walking, running and cycling.
- **Markets**: wander the Carmel, Levinsky and Jaffa flea markets.
- **Street art** in Florentin.
- **The White City**: a self-guided walk past Bauhaus buildings around Rothschild, Dizengoff and Bialik Street.
- **Yarkon Park**: lawns, lakes and paths.
