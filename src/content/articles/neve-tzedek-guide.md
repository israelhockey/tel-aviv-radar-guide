Founded in the 1880s, before Tel Aviv itself, Neve Tzedek is the city's most charming neighbourhood. Its narrow lanes are lined with restored houses, boutiques, galleries and cafés.

## What to do

- **Shabazi Street**: the main lane, full of design shops and cafés.
- **Suzanne Dellal Centre**: the home of Israeli contemporary dance, set around a lovely piazza.
- **HaTachana**: the restored Ottoman-era railway station, now shops and restaurants, at the neighbourhood's edge.
- **Sunset at Charles Clore Park**, a few minutes away by the sea.
