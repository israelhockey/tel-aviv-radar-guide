Tel Aviv is often called the vegan capital of the world, and walking around, it's easy to see why. Almost every menu has vegan dishes marked, and a growing number of restaurants are fully plant-based. Even classics like hummus and falafel are vegan by default.

## Our picks

[[pick: Anastasia | A cosy, fully vegan café on Frishman with a big all-day menu, great cakes and good coffee with plant milks.]]

[[pick: Meshek Barzilay | A long-running vegetarian restaurant in Neve Tzedek with plenty of vegan dishes, especially good for brunch.]]

[[pick: Zakaim | Home-style vegan cooking inspired by family recipes. Warm, generous and full of flavour.]]

[[pick: BANA | Creative plant-based cooking in a modern space. Good for dinner.]]

[[pick: Goodness | Vegan comfort food: burgers, fries and shakes for when you want junk food that happens to be plant-based.]]
