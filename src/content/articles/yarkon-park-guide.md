Yarkon Park (Park HaYarkon) is Tel Aviv's Central Park: a long green strip along the Yarkon River with huge lawns, a lake, sports fields, gardens and miles of paths.

## What to do

- **Cycle or run** the riverside paths, which connect to the coast and the port.
- **Rent a paddle boat** on the lake.
- **Picnic** on the big lawns. On Saturdays and holidays, half the city brings a barbecue.
- **Visit the gardens**, including a rock garden and a tropical garden.
- **Concerts**: big outdoor concerts are held at the park's open-air venue.

## Getting there

From the city centre, cycle north up Ibn Gvirol or along the coast. Tel-O-Fun docks are near the park's entrances; check live bikes on the [map](/live?layers=bikes).
