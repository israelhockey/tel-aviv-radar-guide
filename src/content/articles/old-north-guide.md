The Old North (HaTzafon HaYashan) is where many locals dream of living: quiet tree-lined streets of Bauhaus apartment blocks, neighbourhood cafés, small parks and the sea just a few streets west.

## What to do

- **Dizengoff Street**: boutiques, cafés and people-watching from Dizengoff Square north to the port.
- **Basel Street**: a small, local strip of cafés and shops with a village feel.
- **The port and the promenade**: walk or cycle north along the sea.
- **Yarkon Park**: the city's big green park, just across the river.
