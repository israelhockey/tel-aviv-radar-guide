Halloween isn't a traditional holiday in Israel, but Tel Aviv has adopted it with enthusiasm. Bars throw costume nights, clubs run themed parties, and kids' events pop up across the city. This year it falls on **Saturday 31 October**, so expect the biggest parties on Thursday and Friday night and again on Saturday after Shabbat.

## Where the parties are

- **Florentin and Allenby** have the highest concentration of bars, and many run costume nights.
- **Clubs** go big with themed lineups. Check the [parties calendar](/events?cat=party&range=week).
- **The port** and beach bars often host outdoor events if the weather holds.

## With kids

Community centres and shopping centres often run costume workshops and trick-or-treat style events. Look in the [family calendar](/events?cat=family&range=week).

[[tip: Because Halloween is on Shabbat this year, most public transport won't run on Friday night or during the day on Saturday. The city's free Naim BaSofash shuttles do. See [Shabbat & holidays](/shabbat).]]
