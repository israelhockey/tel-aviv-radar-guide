Tel Aviv goes out late. Dinner at nine, bars at eleven, a club after one, and at some point everyone gets hungry. Luckily the city is full of places that keep cooking long after midnight, especially on Thursday and Friday nights, and a few that barely close at all.

The rules of thumb: Allenby Street and Florentin are your best bets in the south, Dizengoff and Ibn Gvirol in the centre, and the beachfront pubs if you're near the sea. Kosher places are closed on Friday night but often reopen late on Saturday night. And the live list below always shows what's actually open right now.

Each pick links to its own page with exact hours and a map.

## The picks

[[pick: Joe's Pizza | New York slices on Allenby, open until three in the morning early in the week and until five or later towards the weekend. The single most reliable late-night meal in the city.]]

[[pick: Hummus Ashkara | Hummus at two in the morning? Ashkara on Yirmiyahu Street opens at five in the morning and, Sunday to Thursday, stays open until two at night. The best cure for a long night.]]

[[pick: Men Tenten | Ramen on Nahalat Binyamin until one most nights, two on some, and three in the morning on Thursdays. A bowl of hot noodles after the bars is hard to beat.]]

[[pick: Junky Burger | Smash burgers on Levinsky Street in Florentin, until midnight most nights and one in the morning on Thursdays and Saturdays.]]

[[pick: 90's Diner | A diner-style burger joint on Yefet Street in Jaffa, open until midnight in the week and three in the morning on Friday nights.]]

[[pick: Favela | Burgers and sandwiches in the north of the city, open until two in the morning most nights (and from Saturday evening after Shabbat). It's kosher, which makes it rare for this hour.]]

[[pick: Mike's Place | The beachfront pub on Herbert Samuel Street, open until four in the morning every day, with food, beer and sport on the screens.]]

[[pick: Sputnik | Not a restaurant, but if you need one more drink, the sprawling bar on Allenby is open until six in the morning.]]

[[pick: Riva Reno | Italian gelato on Nahalat Binyamin until midnight most nights and two in the morning on Thursdays and Fridays. Dessert counts.]]

[[pick: Robina | A neighbourhood bar on Marmorek Street with food, open until three every night.]]

## FAQ

### Is there 24-hour food in Tel Aviv?

A few places run close to around the clock, and many stay open until three, four or five in the morning on weekend nights. Joe's Pizza on Allenby and Mike's Place on the beachfront are two of the latest.

### Where can I eat after a night out in Florentin?

Junky Burger, the area's pizza places and the bars that serve food until late. The live list above shows what's open right now.

### Is anything kosher open late?

Kosher places close on Friday night. During the week and after Shabbat on Saturday night, some kosher spots stay open late, like Favela in the north of the city.

### Does Tel Aviv have late-night food delivery?

Yes. The delivery apps used in Israel cover many restaurants until late, especially pizza, burgers and sushi.
