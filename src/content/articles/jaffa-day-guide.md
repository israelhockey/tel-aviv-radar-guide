Jaffa is one of the oldest port cities in the world, and the most atmospheric part of Tel Aviv-Yafo. Give it a whole day.

## Morning: the flea market

The **Jaffa Flea Market** (Shuk HaPishpeshim) is a maze of vintage furniture, antiques, clothes and junk, surrounded by cafés and restaurants. It's busiest on Friday mornings.

## Afternoon: old Jaffa and the port

Climb up past the **clock tower** into the old city's stone lanes, galleries and lookouts, then down to the **old port**, now full of restaurants and small shops.

[[photo: city:clocktower | Jaffa's clock tower]]

## Sunset

The view north from the hill of old Jaffa, with the Tel Aviv skyline across the bay, is the classic Tel Aviv sunset. Sunset tonight is on our [beach page](/beach).

## Getting there

Walk or cycle the promenade south from central Tel Aviv, or take the Red Line to Elifelet and walk west.
