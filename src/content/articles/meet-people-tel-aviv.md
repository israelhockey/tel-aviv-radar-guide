Tel Aviv is friendly and direct, and people are used to newcomers. The trick is showing up somewhere regularly. Here are the easiest ways in.

## Meetups and networking

Tech, startups, languages, board games and hobbies: there's a meetup for almost everything, most nights of the week.

[[events: category=meetup; range=week; limit=6]]

## Classes at community centres

The city's community centres run hundreds of classes, from yoga and running groups to art workshops and Hebrew. They're cheap or free, and a great way to meet neighbours.

[[events: category=community; range=week; limit=6]]

## Sport and the beach

- **Run clubs** meet along the promenade and in Yarkon Park.
- **Beach volleyball and matkot** (beach paddle ball) happen on every beach. Ask to join.
- **Cycling**: the bike lanes and Tel-O-Fun bikes make group rides easy.

## Volunteering

Volunteering is one of the fastest ways to feel at home. Food rescue, animal shelters and community kitchens always need hands.
