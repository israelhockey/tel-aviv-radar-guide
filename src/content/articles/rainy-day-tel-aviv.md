It doesn't rain often in Tel Aviv, but when it does, it pours. Here's how to spend a wet day.

- **Museums**: the Tel Aviv Museum of Art, the ANU Museum of the Jewish People and smaller galleries across the city.
- **Cinema**: catch a film at the Cinematheque. See [what's playing](/cinema).
- **Cafés**: settle in with a coffee and a book. See [our coffee guide](/magazine/best-coffee-tel-aviv).
- **Indoor food markets**: Sarona Market has dozens of stalls under one roof.
