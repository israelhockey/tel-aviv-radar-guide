Yom Ha'atzmaut starts the evening right after Memorial Day, so the mood switches from remembrance to celebration at sunset. In Tel Aviv that means fireworks, free concerts and streets full of people.

[[holiday: Yom HaAtzma]]

## The evening

- **Free stages and concerts** in city squares.
- **Fireworks** over the city and the sea.
- **Street parties** that go on late, especially in the centre.

## The day

Israelis celebrate the day itself with a "mangal" (barbecue). Every park, especially Yarkon Park, fills with smoke and families from the morning. Air force flyovers pass along the coast around midday.
