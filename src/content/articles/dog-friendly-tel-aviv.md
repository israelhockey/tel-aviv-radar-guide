Tel Aviv loves dogs. You'll see them in cafés, on buses and at the beach. Here's what you need to know.

## Dog parks

The city has dozens of fenced dog parks ("ginat klavim") across every neighbourhood, many with water fountains and shade. Find the nearest one on the map in our [city guide](/guide).

## The dog beach

There's a dedicated dog beach next to **Hilton Beach**, where dogs are allowed to run and swim. On most other beaches dogs aren't allowed during the bathing season.

## Parks

**Yarkon Park**, the city's biggest green space, is perfect for long walks. Leashes are required in most areas. See our [Yarkon Park guide](/magazine/yarkon-park-guide).

## Cafés and restaurants

Most cafés with outdoor seating welcome dogs, and many bring a water bowl without being asked. Inside is up to each place, so ask.

## Rules

- Dogs must be leashed in public spaces outside dog parks.
- Clean up after your dog: there are bag dispensers in many parks, and fines for not picking up.
- Dogs need to be registered and vaccinated with the city.

[[tip: Summer pavements get very hot. Walk early in the morning or after sunset.]]
