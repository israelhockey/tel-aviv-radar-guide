Tel Aviv's nightlife is famous for good reason: bars are busy every night of the week, clubs run until morning, and the city feels safe and friendly after dark.

## How it works

- **Start late.** Bars fill from 22:00, clubs from midnight.
- **Thursday is the big night**, followed by Friday. Saturday picks up after Shabbat.
- **Dress code** is relaxed almost everywhere.
- **Cash is rarely needed**, and tipping 10–15% at the bar is normal.

## Where to go

- **Florentin**: small, cheap, busy bars. See our [Florentin guide](/magazine/florentin-guide).
- **Allenby and Rothschild**: cocktail bars and clubs within a few blocks.
- **Dizengoff**: classic bars and late-night food.
- **The port and the beach**: outdoor bars in summer.
