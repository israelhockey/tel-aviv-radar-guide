Florentin is Tel Aviv's grittiest, liveliest corner: old workshops and furniture makers by day, packed bars and late kitchens by night, and some of the city's best street art on almost every wall.

## By day

- **Street art**: walk the side streets around Vital and Florentin for murals and graffiti.
- **Levinsky Market**: spices, dried fruit, pickles and Balkan delis, on the neighbourhood's edge.
- **Cafés** fill with freelancers working from laptops.

## Getting there

Florentin sits between Jaffa and the city centre. The Red Line stops at Elifelet nearby, and it's a short ride from Rothschild by bike.
