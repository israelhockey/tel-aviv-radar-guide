Tel Aviv's pizza scene has grown up. You'll find wood-fired Neapolitan pies, thin New York-style slices sold until the early hours, and the old-school Israeli pizzeria with corn and olives on top.

## Our picks

[[pick: Tony Vespa | Slices by weight, with unusual toppings and long hours. The default late-night stop in the centre.]]

[[pick: Brooklyn Pizza | Big, foldable New York slices. Exactly what it says on the sign.]]

[[pick: Pizza Shemesh | The classic Israeli pizzeria. Order a "magash" (a whole tray) for the table and argue about the toppings.]]

## Good to know

- Many pizzerias are kosher and dairy-only, so there's no meat on the menu.
- Late at night, slice shops are the fastest food in town after shawarma.
