Hanukkah is one of the most fun times to be in Tel Aviv. Bakeries compete over the most extravagant sufganiyot (jam doughnuts), public menorahs are lit around the city, and kids are off school, so there are shows and activities everywhere.

[[holiday: Chanukah]]

## What to expect

- **Sufganiyot everywhere**: bakeries start selling them weeks before. Try a classic jam one and something wild.
- **Menorah lightings** in public squares each evening.
- **Kids' shows and festivals**: school is out, so theatres, museums and the city run special programmes.
