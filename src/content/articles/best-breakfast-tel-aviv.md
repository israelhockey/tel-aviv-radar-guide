Breakfast is a big deal here. The classic Israeli breakfast is a feast of eggs, salads, cheeses, bread and dips, and on Friday mornings the whole city seems to be out for brunch.

## Our picks

[[pick: Benedict | Breakfast around the clock. Eggs any way you like them, at 9 in the morning or 3 at night.]]

[[pick: Dr. Shakshuka | A Jaffa institution dedicated to the city's favourite pan of eggs in tomato sauce. Loud, busy and fun.]]

[[pick: Puaa | A café in the Jaffa flea market where the furniture is for sale and the breakfast is excellent. Perfect before a wander through the stalls.]]

[[pick: Meshek Barzilay | A Neve Tzedek favourite for a vegetarian brunch with a menu full of vegan options.]]
