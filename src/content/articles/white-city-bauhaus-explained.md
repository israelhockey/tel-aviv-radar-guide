In 2003 UNESCO declared Tel Aviv's "White City" a World Heritage site. It's home to roughly 4,000 buildings in the International Style, often called Bauhaus, built mostly in the 1930s by architects who had trained in Europe.

## How to spot a Bauhaus building

- **Ribbon windows**: long horizontal windows, often shaded by a concrete strip.
- **Rounded balconies** wrapping around corners.
- **Flat roofs** where residents once gathered on hot evenings.
- **Pilotis**: columns lifting the building off the ground to let the sea breeze through.
- **White or pale plaster**, which gave the White City its name.

Architects adapted European modernism to the Mediterranean climate: smaller windows against the sun, deep balconies for shade, and raised ground floors for air.
