Tel Aviv's cocktail scene regularly lands on international best-bar lists, and the city's bars stay busy every night of the week. Evenings start late: 22:00 is early.

## Our picks

[[pick: Bellboy | Theatrical, playful cocktails served with a lot of showmanship. Book for a special night.]]

[[pick: Spicehaus | Drinks built around spices and Middle Eastern ingredients, on Dizengoff. Inventive without being fussy.]]

[[pick: 223 Bar | A classic cocktail bar with a long history in the city. Dim lights, serious bartenders, perfectly made classics.]]

[[pick: Double Standard | A small bar with a big reputation among bartenders. Creative drinks, relaxed vibe.]]

[[pick: Port Said | Not strictly a cocktail bar, but the restaurant-bar next to the Great Synagogue is one of the best places in the city to drink outdoors, with a DJ and a crowd that spills onto the street.]]

[[tip: Many bars run a happy hour in the early evening. Our [Food & drink page](/food) shows the ones happening right now.]]
