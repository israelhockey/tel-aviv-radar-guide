Tel Aviv is famous as one of the most welcoming cities in the world for LGBTQ+ travellers. The scene isn't confined to one district: it's woven through the whole city, from beach to bar.

## Pride

Tel Aviv Pride has traditionally been held in June and is the largest in the Middle East, with a parade, a beach party and a week of events. Dates and plans change year to year, so check [What's on](/events) closer to the time.

## The beach

**Hilton Beach** is the city's best-known gay beach, beside the breakwater near the Hilton hotel. It's relaxed and mixed, with a dog beach and surfers nearby. See our [beaches guide](/magazine/tel-aviv-beaches-guide).

## Gan Meir and the community centre

**Gan Meir**, the small park off King George Street, is home to the city's LGBTQ community centre, with events, support services and a café. The streets around it are a good starting point for a night out.

## Nightlife

The city has bars and parties every night of the week, and many of the biggest parties are LGBTQ+ nights at mainstream clubs. Line-ups change constantly, so the best way to find them is our [events calendar](/events?cat=party) and the [nightlife guide](/magazine/nightlife-guide-tel-aviv).

## Good to know

- Same-sex couples are a normal sight in Tel Aviv, and hotels and restaurants are welcoming.
- Attitudes are more conservative in religious areas and in some other parts of the country.
