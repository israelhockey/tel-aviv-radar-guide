Hummus here is a morning-to-lunch food. The best places make one big batch, serve it warm, and close when it runs out. Go hungry, go early, and don't expect a long menu.

## How to order like a local

- **Hummus** comes plain, with whole chickpeas, or with ful (stewed fava beans).
- **Msabbaha** is warm whole chickpeas in tahini. Order it if you see it.
- Bread, raw onion, pickles and hot sauce come on the side. Tear, scoop, repeat.

## Our picks

[[pick: Abu Hassan | The name everyone mentions, in Jaffa. Shared tables, fast service, and a bowl that is famously smooth. Get there before lunch; when it's gone, it's gone.]]

[[pick: Hummus Ashkara | A north Tel Aviv institution near the Yarkon with long hours, so it's the answer when the craving hits at night.]]

[[pick: Garger HaZahav | A small, friendly hummus spot with generous bowls and good extras. A favourite for a cheap, filling lunch.]]

[[pick: Hummus Abu Adham | Another Jaffa favourite, a little calmer than the big names, with excellent warm chickpeas.]]
