The Red Line, Tel Aviv's first light rail line, opened in 2023. It runs from Petah Tikva in the east, through central Tel Aviv underground, and down through Jaffa to Bat Yam in the south. More lines are being built.

## The stations you'll use most

- **Savidor Center (Arlozorov):** change for trains, including to the airport and Jerusalem.
- **Shaul HaMelech:** for the Tel Aviv Museum of Art and Sarona.
- **Yehudit:** near Sarona and Azrieli.
- **Carlebach:** for the city centre and Rothschild's north end.
- **Allenby:** the closest stop to the Carmel Market, Rothschild and the White City.
- **Elifelet:** for Florentin and Jaffa's northern edge.
- South of the centre, the line runs above ground through Jaffa towards Bat Yam.

## How to pay

Use a Rav-Kav card or a public transport payment app (such as Moovit or Rav-Pass), and validate before you board. Fares are the same as buses in the city, and transfers to buses are included within the time limit.

## When it runs

Trains run frequently from early morning until late at night, Sunday to Friday afternoon. The light rail doesn't run on Shabbat. See [this week's Shabbat times](/shabbat).

## Tips

- Check live departures in Moovit.
- The underground stations are deep and also serve as protected spaces.
- For short hops in the centre, walking or a Tel-O-Fun bike is often just as quick.
