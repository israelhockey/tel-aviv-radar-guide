Tel Aviv is a largely secular city, but there are hundreds of kosher restaurants, cafés and bakeries. Here's what to know.

## How kosher works here

- A restaurant's certificate (teudat kashrut) hangs near the entrance. Ask to see it if you're unsure.
- Kosher places serve either meat or dairy, never both. Dairy places are often cafés and pizzerias.
- Most kosher places close on Friday afternoon and reopen on Saturday night after Shabbat.
