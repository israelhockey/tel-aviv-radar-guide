Tel Aviv has a Mediterranean climate: hot, humid summers and mild winters with most of the year's rain falling between November and March. For today's forecast and sea conditions, see the [beach page](/beach).

## Spring (March to May)

Warm, sunny days and cooler evenings, with wildflowers in the parks. The sea starts to warm up in May. It's one of the best times to visit, but Passover and Independence Day bring crowds and higher prices.

## Summer (June to September)

Hot and humid, with daytime highs around 30°C and warm nights. It almost never rains. The sea is warm, festivals and beach parties are everywhere, and life moves outdoors after dark. Midday in the sun can be tough: swim early or late.

## Autumn (October to November)

Still warm, with the sea at its warmest in early autumn. The first rains usually arrive in October or November. The High Holidays fall in September or October and change the city's rhythm for a few weeks. A great time for a beach holiday without the summer heat.

## Winter (December to February)

Mild: often sunny days in the high teens °C, cooler nights, and spells of heavy rain and storms. Some people still swim. It's the quietest season, with good hotel deals, Hanukkah and Christmas in Jaffa. Pack a rain jacket. Our [rainy day guide](/magazine/rainy-day-tel-aviv) has ideas.

## The short version

- **Best for the beach:** June to October.
- **Best all-round:** April to June and September to November.
- **Cheapest:** winter, outside the holidays.

[[tip: The sun is strong all year. Check the UV index on our beach page before a long day outside.]]
