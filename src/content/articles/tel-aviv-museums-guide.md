Tel Aviv's museums are a good reason to come in out of the sun.

- **Tel Aviv Museum of Art**: Israel's major modern art museum, in a striking building on Shaul HaMelech, with a big international collection.
- **ANU, the Museum of the Jewish People**: in Ramat Aviv, telling Jewish stories from around the world.
- **Eretz Israel Museum**: archaeology, crafts and history across a large campus in Ramat Aviv.
- **Independence Hall**: on Rothschild Boulevard, where the state was declared in 1948.
- **The Bauhaus Center**: a small space about the White City, the start of our [Bauhaus walk](/walks/white-city-bauhaus).
- **Galleries in Jaffa and Neve Tzedek**: dozens of small galleries, many free.

Many museums are open on Saturday. See [what's open on Shabbat](/shabbat).
