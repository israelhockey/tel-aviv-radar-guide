Israel's craft beer scene has grown fast, and Tel Aviv is where you can taste it all. These are the bars with the best taps and the friendliest pints.

## Our picks

[[pick: Beer Bazaar | A champion of Israeli craft beer, with dozens of local brews. A great introduction to what the country's breweries are making.]]

[[pick: Porter & Sons | A huge beer list, food that soaks it up and room for a group.]]

[[pick: Dancing Camel Pub | The pub of one of Israel's first craft breweries. Try something brewed down the road.]]

[[pick: Beer Garden | Outdoor tables and cold beer. Exactly what a warm Tel Aviv evening needs.]]
