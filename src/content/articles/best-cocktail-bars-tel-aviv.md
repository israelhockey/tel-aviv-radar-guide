Tel Aviv drinks late and drinks well. In the last decade the city has built one of the most exciting cocktail scenes in the Mediterranean, with bartenders who travel the world's bar shows and bring back ideas, and a love of local ingredients: za'atar, arak, date honey, sumac, citrus from the market that morning.

The rhythm matters. Nobody goes out early here. Bars fill from ten, peak around midnight and many stay open until two or three, later on Thursday and Friday. Thursday is the big night of the week. Many bars offer happy hour deals in the early evening, and most don't take reservations, so arrive early if you want a seat at the bar.

## The cocktail bars

[[pick: Bellboy | The most theatrical cocktail bar in town, in the Bellboy hotel on Berdichevsky Street. Drinks arrive in teapots, tins, bird cages and things you won't have seen before, and they taste as good as they look. It's a little bit of a show, so come for a special occasion or to impress a visitor.]]

[[pick: Spicehaus | An intimate cocktail bar on Dizengoff built around spices and botanicals. Open until one most nights and two at the weekend. Sit at the bar and let the bartenders recommend something: the menu changes with the seasons.]]

[[pick: 223 Bar | A long-running cocktail bar at Dizengoff 223, open until two (three on Thursdays and Fridays). Classic drinks, made properly, in a dark, cosy room. A favourite of people who care about a well-made Negroni.]]

[[pick: Double Standard | A cocktail bar towards the northern end of Dizengoff with a creative menu and a lively crowd. Good for a second stop after dinner in the Old North.]]

[[pick: Port Said | Not strictly a cocktail bar, but no Tel Aviv drinking list is complete without it. Next to the Great Synagogue on Har Sinai Street, Port Said spills onto the pavement every night, with records on the turntable, simple food and a crowd that's half the city's creative scene. Go after ten.]]

[[pick: The Prince | A rooftop bar above Nahalat Binyamin with a laid-back, slightly ramshackle charm and tables outside. Perfect on a warm evening, and open from midday at the weekend.]]

[[pick: Robina | A neighbourhood cocktail bar on Marmorek Street with outdoor seating, open until three every night. Relaxed, friendly and good for a late drink without the club crowd.]]

[[pick: Bushwick | A small bar on Nahalat Binyamin with a cool, laid-back feel, open every evening. A good first stop on a night around the Carmel Market area.]]

[[pick: Helena | A bar on Tarsat Boulevard near Habima, open every evening and from lunchtime at the weekend. Good drinks in a stylish setting near the theatre and concert halls.]]

[[pick: Jazz Kissa | A Japanese-style listening bar where the music is the point: great records on a serious sound system, and drinks to match. Small and atmospheric. Go to listen, not to shout over the music.]]

## What to drink

- **Arak:** the anise spirit of the Levant, traditionally with water and ice or with grapefruit juice. Many bars use it in cocktails.
- **Local gin:** Israel has several craft distilleries, and their gins appear on many menus.
- **Israeli wine:** wine bars are everywhere. Ask for something from the Golan or the Judean Hills.
- **Beer:** see [the best beer bars](/magazine/best-beer-bars-tel-aviv).

## FAQ

### What is the best cocktail bar in Tel Aviv?

Bellboy is the most famous and the most theatrical. Spicehaus and 223 are favourites for beautifully made drinks without the show. Port Said is the city's most loved bar, cocktails or not.

### What time do bars open and close in Tel Aviv?

Most bars open in the early evening and get busy from ten. Closing is usually between one and three, later on Thursday and Friday. Some, like Sputnik on Allenby, stay open until morning.

### What is the best night to go out in Tel Aviv?

Thursday is the biggest night of the week, followed by Friday. Bars are busy every night in summer.

### Are bars open on Shabbat in Tel Aviv?

Yes, most bars in Tel Aviv are open on Friday night and Saturday. Some kosher bars close for Shabbat.

### How much does a cocktail cost in Tel Aviv?

Tel Aviv is one of the more expensive cities in the region, and cocktails are priced accordingly. Happy hour in the early evening is the best way to save.
