One of Tel Aviv's great free shows happens every evening: the sun dropping into the Mediterranean. Get there 20 minutes early, and check today's sunset time on the [home page](/).

## Abrasha Park, Old Jaffa

The hilltop park above the old port has the classic view north along the coast to the Tel Aviv skyline, which glows as the sun goes down. Our [Old Jaffa walk](/walks/old-jaffa) ends nearby.

## The Hilton breakwater

The stone breakwater at Hilton Beach juts into the sea, with surfers below and the city behind you.

## Gordon and Frishman beaches

Grab a spot on the sand, or a drink at a beach bar, and watch the volleyball players and matkot games as the light turns gold.

## Tel Aviv Port

The wooden deck at the Port faces west. Walk it at sunset, then stay for dinner or drinks.

## Charles Clore Park

Lawns by the sea between the city and Jaffa, perfect for a sunset picnic away from the busiest beaches.

## The promenade

Anywhere along the promenade works. Walk south towards Jaffa as the sun sets for a view of the old city lit up against the sky.

[[tip: Bring a layer in winter: it gets cool by the sea as soon as the sun goes down.]]
