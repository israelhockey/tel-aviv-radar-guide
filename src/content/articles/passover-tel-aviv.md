Passover (Pesach) is a week-long spring holiday. Families gather for the Seder on the first night, many shops and restaurants change their menus to avoid leavened bread, and lots of people take the week off to travel.

[[holiday: Pesach I]]

## What to expect

- **Seder night**: almost everything closes early on the first evening. Book a seder or join friends.
- **The first and last days** work like Shabbat: no buses or trains, and most businesses close.
- **The days in between** (chol hamoed) are a holiday week with family events, crowded beaches and parks, and busy roads.
- **Bread** is hard to find in supermarkets and kosher places. Many non-kosher restaurants and cafés in Tel Aviv serve it as usual.
